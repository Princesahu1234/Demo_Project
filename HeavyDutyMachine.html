Gun Drilling Machines

Global Partner: Dezhou Tianrui Machinery
Key Models & Series: ZK-21 Series (3-Axis & Full Protection)
⚙

How It Works

Employs single-lip gun drills with internal coolant channels. The tool rotates while high-pressure oil is fed through the center of the drill to cool the cutting edge and push chips out through a V-shaped fluted groove.

★

Key Advantages

  • Superior Hole Finish: Generates fine surface roughness (Ra 0.4–1.6 µm).
  • Tight Tolerances: Achieves H7-H9 hole accuracy in a single pass.
  • Full Enclosure Safety: Ergonomic guarding contains high-pressure mist and chips.

Skiving & Roller Burnishing Systems

⚙

How It Works

Combines two mechanical steps into a single pass: peeling/skiving heads shave off internal stock allowance at high speed, followed immediately by roller burnishing heads that cold-work and smooth the surface via plastic deformation.

★

Key Advantages

  • Enhanced Surface Hardness: Roller burnishing work-hardens the inner skin to resist wear.
  • Mirror Finish: Consistently achieves Ra < 0.2 µm.
⚡

Latest Technology

  • Automatic tool retraction and expansion mechanism.
  • High-torque spindle drives with intelligent chip breaking.
  • Integrated hydraulic clamping and rapid part loading.

Adaptive Drilling Control (ADC)

2x Longer Tool Life
